How to play

Fly through an endless asteroid field and shoot everything in your way. Hitting an asteroid scores points, and destroying it scores more. Big asteroids break into smaller pieces.

Crashing into asteroids costs health, more for bigger ones. Collect ammo drops for the laser (max 100) and the cannon (max 30), and health drops to repair your ship.

The field gets harder as your score climbs, and enemy ships start hunting you at higher difficulty.
